\noindent\textbf{Highlights}

\vspace{6pt}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=4pt]
\item Under full AGI substitution, market employment falls monotonically to zero.
\item A high market-employment endpoint is unreachable and read as a counterfactual.
\item Full public-employment absorption gives two basins: crash or smooth transition.
\item The critical frontier growth is 0.095 at tau=0.15, saturating near 0.14.
\item On the smooth path, minimum consumption is 0.407 and residual unemployment is zero.
\end{itemize}
